Barbarian subclass

Battlerager

Battleragers are reckless front-line Barbarians who make armor part of the attack instead of only defense.

The path focuses on spiked armor, grappling pressure, temporary hit points, and staying dangerous when enemies strike back.

Later features such as Battlerager Armor, Reckless Abandon, Battlerager Charge expand that identity at higher levels, so the subclass has a clearer role in combat, exploration, utility, or support.
