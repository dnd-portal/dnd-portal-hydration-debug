About the Barbarian

Barbarians channel primal forces through Rage, but that power is more than ordinary anger.

Different Barbarians understand Rage through spirits, ancestors, pain, wild magic, nature, or the deepest part of the self breaking through.

Rage sharpens a Barbarian's combat ability, reflexes, senses, and raw physical force. In an adventuring party, Barbarians often become protectors and leaders because they willingly enter danger first and hold the line for everyone behind them.

FAQ

What is a Barbarian in D&D?

A Barbarian is a durable martial class built around Rage, a limited combat state that improves Strength-based offense and makes the character much harder to bring down. Barbarians use a d12 Hit Die, are proficient with simple and martial weapons, and usually fight on the front line with high Strength and Constitution.

How do you play a Barbarian in D&D?

Play a Barbarian by entering Rage when its damage and resistance will matter, attacking mainly with Strength-based melee weapons, and using your durability to pressure dangerous enemies. Reckless Attack can secure advantage when accuracy is worth the defensive risk, so good Barbarian play is about timing, positioning, and choosing when to commit rather than simply charging every enemy.

What is the Hit Die for a Barbarian?

A Barbarian uses a d12 Hit Die for every Barbarian level in the 2014 rules. At 1st level, a Barbarian starts with 12 + their Constitution modifier hit points; at later Barbarian levels they add 1d12, or the fixed average of 7, plus their Constitution modifier. Hit Dice can also be spent to recover hit points during short rests.

How does Rage work for a Barbarian?

In the 2014 rules, a Barbarian enters Rage as a bonus action. While raging and not wearing heavy armor, the Barbarian has advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws, deals extra damage with qualifying Strength-based melee weapon attacks, and has resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage. Rage prevents spellcasting and concentration and normally lasts up to 1 minute, subject to its early-ending rules.

What ability scores are best for a Barbarian?

Strength is usually the Barbarian's highest ability score because it drives the attacks that work best with Rage and Reckless Attack and supports Athletics. Constitution is normally the second priority because it increases hit points and contributes to Unarmored Defense, while Dexterity improves Armor Class, initiative, and Dexterity saving throws. The 2014 multiclass prerequisite for Barbarian is Strength 13.
