Class Features

As a fighter, you gain the following class features.

Fighting Style

Level 1

Choose a persistent combat specialization such as Archery, Defense, Dueling, Great Weapon Fighting, Protection, Two-Weapon Fighting, or a Tasha’s option.

Second Wind

Level 1

Use a bonus action D&D Portal - Bonus Action Action rule Bonus Action is an action economy rule used to describe what a creature can do on its turn. Full link: https://dnd-portal.com/rules/actions/bonus-action/ once per short or long rest D&D Portal - Long Rest Rest rule Long Rest is a rest rule used to recover resources, hit points, and class features. Full link: https://dnd-portal.com/rules/rests/long-rest/ to recover 1d10 + fighter level hit points D&D Portal - Hit Points Combat rule Hit Points is a combat rule used by attacks, damage, defenses, or turn order. Full link: https://dnd-portal.com/rules/combat/hit-points/.

Action Surge

Levels 2, second use at 17

Take one additional action on the current turn; recover uses on a short or long rest D&D Portal - Long Rest Rest rule Long Rest is a rest rule used to recover resources, hit points, and class features. Full link: https://dnd-portal.com/rules/rests/long-rest/.

Martial Archetype

Levels 3, 7, 10, 15, 18

Choose a subclass that defines tactical, magical, or weapon specialization.

Ability Score Improvement

Levels 4, 6, 8, 12, 14, 16, 19

Gain more ASIs D&D Portal - ASI Shortened rule term ASI means Ability Score Improvement. Use an ASI when a class feature grants Ability Score Improvement. You normally increase one ability score by 2, or two ability scores by 1 each, within the normal maximum. If feats are allowed, the ASI can often be exchanged for a feat. Full link: https://dnd-portal.com/rules/abbreviations/asi/ than any other base class.

Extra Attack

Levels 5, 11, 20

Attack two, then three, then four times with the Attack action D&D Portal - Attack Action Action rule Attack Action is an action economy rule used to describe what a creature can do on its turn. Full link: https://dnd-portal.com/rules/actions/attack-action/.

Levels 9, 13, 17

Reroll a failed saving throw D&D Portal - Saving Throw D20 test rule Saving Throw is a d20 test rule used to resolve uncertain outcomes during play. Full link: https://dnd-portal.com/rules/d20-tests/saving-throw/; uses increase at higher levels.

FAQ

What is a Fighter in D&D?

A Fighter is a weapon-focused martial class built around broad armor and weapon proficiency, a Fighting Style, Second Wind, Action Surge, Extra Attack, frequent Ability Score Improvements, and Indomitable. Fighters use a d10 Hit Die and can specialise in Strength or Dexterity, melee or ranged combat, defense, tactics, or subclass-specific magic.

How do you play a Fighter?

Play a Fighter by choosing a coherent weapon and armor plan, matching it with a Fighting Style, and using Action Surge when an extra action can change the encounter rather than spending it automatically. Strength or Dexterity should match the chosen weapons, Constitution supports durability, and the Martial Archetype determines whether the Fighter stays straightforward or adds manoeuvres, magic, runes, psionics, or another specialist system.

What ability scores are best for a Fighter?

A Fighter normally makes either Strength or Dexterity the highest ability score, depending on the weapons and armor being used. Constitution is usually the second major priority because it increases hit points. Strength is best for heavy melee weapons and many heavy-armor builds; Dexterity is best for ranged and finesse weapons and also improves initiative. In the 2014 multiclassing rules, Fighter requires Strength 13 or Dexterity 13.

What are the main Fighter class features?

The 2014 Fighter's main features are Fighting Style, Second Wind, Action Surge, a Martial Archetype, Extra Attack, extra Ability Score Improvements, and Indomitable. Fighters gain more attacks and more ASI opportunities than most classes, while Action Surge provides short-rest burst action economy and the Martial Archetype supplies the class's specialist mechanics.

What subclasses can a Fighter choose?

A Fighter's subclass is called a Martial Archetype and is chosen at 3rd level in the 2014 rules. Major official pre-2024 Fifth Edition options include Champion, Battle Master, Eldritch Knight, Purple Dragon Knight/Banneret, Arcane Archer, Cavalier, Samurai, Echo Knight, Psi Warrior, and Rune Knight. Each keeps the Fighter's core Action Surge and Extra Attack chassis while adding a different tactical system.
