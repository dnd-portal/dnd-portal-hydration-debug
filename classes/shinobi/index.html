Shinobi

Shinobi are trained operatives who combine physical discipline with the supernatural manipulation of chakra.

They move through the battlefield at extreme speed, deceive enemies with transformations and illusions, and unleash techniques shaped by elemental affinities or inherited bloodlines.

A Shinobi is defined by two major choices: Chakra Origin determines the nature or heritage of their chakra, while Shinobi Path determines how that power is trained and applied.

What is a Shinobi in D&D?

The Shinobi is a D&D Portal homebrew martial-hybrid class built around Dexterity, Wisdom, Chakra Points, supernatural Techniques, a Chakra Origin, and a Shinobi Path. It uses a d8 Hit Die, has Dexterity and Intelligence saving throw proficiencies, wears light armor, and combines fast weapon combat with Rank I–V techniques. Chakra returns on a short or long rest, while a separate Fortune Dice system lets the Shinobi influence important d20 rolls.

How do you play a Shinobi?

Play a Shinobi as a mobile striker and specialist. Keep Dexterity high for weapons, initiative, and movement-based play, and Wisdom high because it powers Techniques. Use the free Shinobi Step to Dash, Disengage, or Hide without spending Chakra, preserve Chakra for Techniques that materially change the encounter, and use Fortune Dice on important d20 rolls rather than trivial ones. Your Chakra Origin and Shinobi Path should work together but remain mechanically separate.

What ability scores are best for a Shinobi?

Dexterity and Wisdom are the Shinobi's primary abilities. Dexterity improves qualifying weapon attacks, Armor Class, initiative, and mobility skills, while Wisdom powers Technique attack rolls and save DCs. Constitution is normally the next priority for hit points and concentration. The current Shinobi multiclassing requirement is Dexterity 13 and Wisdom 13.
