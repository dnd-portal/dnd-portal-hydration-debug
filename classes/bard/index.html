FAQ

What is a Bard in D&D?

A Bard is a Charisma-based full spellcaster and skill-focused support class that combines magic, Bardic Inspiration, broad proficiencies, Expertise, and social ability. Bards use a d8 Hit Die, can fill many different party roles, and choose a Bard College at 3rd level to specialise in areas such as knowledge, martial combat, enchantment, performance, or other forms of magical artistry.

How do you play a Bard?

Play a Bard by using Charisma-based spellcasting, Bardic Inspiration, and strong skills to improve the party rather than focusing only on personal damage. Decide which enemies need controlling, which allies need support, and when an Inspiration die is worth spending. Your Bard College, spell choices, Expertise skills, and concentration management determine whether you lean toward support, control, social play, skill expertise, or martial combat.

What ability scores are best for a Bard?

Charisma is normally the Bard's highest ability score because it powers Bard spellcasting and Bardic Inspiration. Dexterity is usually the next priority because it improves Armor Class, initiative, Dexterity saving throws, and common finesse or ranged attacks. Constitution is also important for hit points and concentration. In the 2014 multiclassing rules, a Bard requires Charisma 13.

What are the main Bard class features?

The 2014 Bard's main features are Spellcasting, Bardic Inspiration, Jack of All Trades, Song of Rest, Expertise, a Bard College, Font of Inspiration, Countercharm, Magical Secrets, and Superior Inspiration. Together they make the Bard a full caster, skill expert, and support character whose strength comes from improving allies, controlling encounters, and adapting to many different situations.

What subclasses can a Bard choose?

A Bard's subclass is called a Bard College and is chosen at 3rd level in the 2014 rules. Official pre-2024 Fifth Edition options include College of Lore, Valor, Glamour, Swords, Whispers, Eloquence, Creation, and Spirits. Each keeps the Bard's core spellcasting, Bardic Inspiration, skills, and Magical Secrets while changing how the class specialises.
