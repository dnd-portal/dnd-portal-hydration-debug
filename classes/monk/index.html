Class Features

As a monk, you gain the following class features.

While unarmored and without a shield D&D Portal - Shields Equipment reference Shields is an equipment reference used by character options and rules. Full link: https://dnd-portal.com/rules/ability-scores/wisdom/ modifier.

Martial Arts

Level 1

Use Dexterity D&D Portal - Dexterity Agility, reflexes, and coordination Dexterity measures agility, reflexes, balance, and precise physical coordination. Full link: https://dnd-portal.com/rules/ability-scores/dexterity/ for monk weapons and unarmed strikes, scale damage with the Martial Arts die, and make a bonus unarmed strike after attacking.

Ki

Level 2

Spend ki points, recovered on a short or long rest D&D Portal - Long Rest Rest rule Long Rest is a rest rule used to recover resources, hit points, and class features. Full link: https://dnd-portal.com/rules/rests/long-rest/, on Flurry of Blows, Patient Defense, Step of the Wind, and later features.

Unarmored Movement

Levels 2, improves through 18

Gain speed D&D Portal - Speed Movement rule Speed is a movement rule used during exploration, encounters, and combat. Full link: https://dnd-portal.com/rules/movement/speed/ while unarmored; at 9th level move across vertical surfaces and liquids during the turn.

Dedicated Weapon (Optional)

Level 2

Optional

Designate a proficient non-heavy, non-special weapon as a monk weapon after a rest.

Monastic Tradition

Levels 3, 6, 11, 17

Choose a subclass that defines the monk’s ki techniques.

Deflect Missiles

Level 3

Use a reaction D&D Portal - Reaction Action rule Reaction is an action economy rule used to describe what a creature can do on its turn. Full link: https://dnd-portal.com/rules/actions/reaction/ to reduce ranged weapon damage and potentially throw the missile back.

Ki-Fueled Attack (Optional)

Level 3

Optional

After spending ki during the action, make a bonus unarmed or monk-weapon attack.

Slow Fall

Level 4

Use a reaction D&D Portal - Reaction Action rule Reaction is an action economy rule used to describe what a creature can do on its turn. Full link: https://dnd-portal.com/rules/actions/reaction/ to reduce falling D&D Portal - Falling Movement rule Falling is a movement rule used during exploration, encounters, and combat. Quickened Healing (Optional)

Level 4

Optional

FAQ

What is a Monk in D&D?

A Monk is a Dexterity- and Wisdom-based martial class built around unarmored combat, Martial Arts, ki, speed, and battlefield mobility. Monks use a d8 Hit Die, gain Unarmored Defense and Martial Arts at 1st level, unlock ki and increased movement at 2nd level, and choose a Monastic Tradition at 3rd level. They are mobile skirmishers rather than heavily armored front-line tanks.

How do you play a Monk?

Play a Monk as a mobile skirmisher: keep Dexterity and Wisdom high, use movement to reach useful targets, and spend ki according to the situation. Flurry of Blows increases offense, Patient Defense improves survival, Step of the Wind improves mobility, and Stunning Strike can control a priority enemy. Avoid treating every ki point as automatic damage or standing still like a heavily armored Fighter.

What ability scores are best for a Monk?

Dexterity is normally the Monk's highest ability score because it powers most Martial Arts attacks and improves Armor Class and initiative. Wisdom is usually the second priority because it also improves Unarmored Defense and sets the saving throw DC for many ki features. Constitution supports hit points. In the 2014 multiclassing rules, a Monk requires Dexterity 13 and Wisdom 13.
