As a blood hunter, you gain the following class features.

Gain advantage to track and recall information about fey, fiends, and undead, and establish the Hemocraft save DC D&D Portal - Difficulty Class D20 test rule Difficulty Class is a d20 test rule used to resolve uncertain outcomes during play. Fighting Style

Level 2

Choose Archery, Dueling, Great Weapon Fighting, or Two-Weapon Fighting.

Levels 2, improves at 7, 14

Take Hemocraft-die damage to imbue a held weapon with extra elemental or esoteric damage.

Blood Hunter Order

Levels 3, 7, 11, 15, 18

Choose a subclass order.

Levels 4, 8, 12, 16, 19

Increase ability scores or take a feat.

Brand a creature hit by a rite weapon to track it and punish damage it deals nearby.
