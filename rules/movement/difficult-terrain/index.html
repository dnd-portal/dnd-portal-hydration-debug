Difficult Terrain

Difficult Terrain is a movement rule used during exploration, encounters, and combat.

What It Is

Difficult Terrain is a movement rule.

This page is a compact play reference. If a spell, feature, monster, item, or DM ruling gives a more specific instruction, use that specific instruction.

How To Use It

Use it when a creature moves during exploration, encounters, or combat.

Limits And Rolls

  • Movement can be changed by terrain, conditions, speed, spells, and features.
  • You only roll when the rule, DM, spell, item, or feature asks for a roll.
